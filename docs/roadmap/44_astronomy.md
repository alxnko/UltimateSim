# Phase 44: Astronomy, Astrology & Celestial Mechanics

## Overview
The sky is a massive, untouchable system that dictates time, tides, and magic.

## Architecture & DOD Implementation
- **Celestial Simulation**: The `CalendarSystem` tracks the orbit of moons, planets, and constellations.
- **Tidal Shifts**: Moon phases directly alter the water levels in coastal biomes (Phase 39), stranding ships or flooding beaches.
- **Astrological Magic**: Esoteric Rituals (Phase 30) check the celestial array. A ritual performed during a solar eclipse receives a 10x multiplier in potency, making these rare events highly contested by rival cults.
