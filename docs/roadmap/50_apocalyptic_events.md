# Phase 50: The Final Entropy (Apocalyptic Events)

## Overview
The ultimate test of the simulation. What happens when the world literally ends?

## Architecture & DOD Implementation
- **Cataclysmic Triggers**: A meteor strike, the awakening of a sealed Megabeast (Phase 28), or a continent-sinking flood (Phase 39).
- **Global Migration**: These events destroy massive swaths of the `MapGrid`. Entire nations are forced into a singular `WanderSystem` AI state, fleeing to the last remaining safe zones.
- **The Crucible of Survival**: Massive overcrowding spikes food prices, triggering hyper-inflation, immediate Resource Wars, Plagues, and complete societal collapse—proving that the ECS simulation can handle the absolute extremes of entropy and survival without scripted game-overs.
