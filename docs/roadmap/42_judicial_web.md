# Phase 42: The Judicial Web & Legal Precedents

## Overview
The `JusticeSystem` (Phase 18) evolves from simple guard responses into a complex, bureaucratic web of trials, juries, and prison breaks.

## Architecture & DOD Implementation
- **Trials & Evidence**: High-tier jurisdictions hold trials before executions. A criminal can use their `Charisma` or spend positive `SparseHookGraph` connections on a Judge to sway the verdict.
- **Incarceration vs. Banishment**: Instead of just fleeing, criminals can be physically locked in `PrisonCell` entities. They must be fed, generating a tax burden on the city.
- **Prison Breaks**: Players or criminal syndicates can use lockpicks (Action-RPG mechanics) or bribes to physically break inmates out of jail, generating massive `CrimeMarkers`.
